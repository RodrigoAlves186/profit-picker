## English

**App name** (max 30): Profit Picker

**App card subtitle** (max 62):
Highlight the best variant with a badge on the product page

**App introduction** (max 100):
Guide shoppers to the variant you want them to buy with a clear badge right on the variant picker.

**App details** (max 500):
Profit Picker lets you choose, for each product, the variant you want customers to pick, such as the size with the best margin or the color you have most stock of. The app adds a badge in your colors right under that option in the variant picker, so shoppers notice it at a glance. Pick the variant and style the badge from the Shopify admin, then turn on the app embed in your theme editor. No code, no theme edits, and it works with Online Store 2.0 themes.

**Features** (max 80 each):
1. Choose which variant gets highlighted on each product
2. Custom badge text with background and text colors
3. Badge appears right on the variant picker of your theme
4. One-click setup as an app embed, no code changes
5. Free plan for up to 5 products

**Search terms** (max 20 each):
variant badge · product badge · variant highlight · best seller badge · upsell

**Demo / test instructions for the reviewer:**
1. Install the app and open it from Apps > Profit Picker.
2. Click "Activate in theme" and save the theme editor to turn on the Profit Picker app embed.
3. Open any product with variants, choose a variant, set the badge text and colors, and click Save.
4. Visit that product page on the storefront: the badge appears under the chosen variant.
5. Plans: open "Plans" and pick a paid plan to see the Shopify charge approval (test charge on development stores).
